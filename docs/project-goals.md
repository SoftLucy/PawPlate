# Project goals

## Reference target

An affordable, built-in two-zone domino induction hob in the class of the K&H 30-IN-3600SL (reported maximum combined input around 3.4 kW). Verify specifications from manufacturer documentation before treating them as design requirements.

## Goals

- Open and reproducible design, with documented hardware and software.
- Repairable modules and documented interfaces.
- Sensible component availability and a transparent bill of materials.
- Research-first development with no required initial spending.
- Clearly documented safety requirements and verification evidence.

## Non-goals for the first milestone

- Building or selling an appliance immediately.
- Sacrificing or modifying the reference hob.
- Claiming compliance, cooking performance, or safety before testing.

## First milestone

Produce a referenced research and requirements document, compare candidate power-sharing architectures, and identify major safety and cost constraints before selecting a circuit topology.
