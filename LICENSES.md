# Licensing plan

No license has been selected yet. Before accepting contributions or publishing design files, choose explicit licenses for hardware design files, firmware/software, and documentation. Until then, do not imply that the repository is already licensed for reuse.


## Third-party reference material

The research notes may link to manufacturer reference designs and public repositories. A public link or downloadable archive is not, by itself, permission to copy, modify, or redistribute its contents. Keep third-party source files, CAD, Gerbers, firmware, images, and other assets out of this repository unless the applicable file-level license or written permission has been verified. Cite the original source and summarize findings instead of mirroring protected files.

See `docs/research/open-reference-design-licensing.md` for the current survey. It is research documentation, not a legal opinion.
