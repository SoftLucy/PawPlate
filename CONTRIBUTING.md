# Contributing

Early contributions that help most: sourced research, architecture comparisons, simulations, safety requirements, component availability checks, mechanical concepts, and realistic cost models.

- Cite primary sources where possible and include access dates.
- Label assumptions and uncertainty.
- Distinguish calculated, simulated, reviewed, and physically tested results.
- Do not submit claims of safety or compliance without supporting verification evidence.
- Keep the design modular and document trade-offs in `decisions/`.
